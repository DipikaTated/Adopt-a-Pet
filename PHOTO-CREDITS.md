# Animal photo credits

The sample animal photos in `public/animal-photos/` were selected from the Pexels links supplied by the project owner. They are organized in `dogs/`, `cats/`, `rabbits/`, and `birds/` and are used as local images in the demo listings.

Pexels photos are provided under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/). The image URLs remain in the project conversation for reference; filenames use the Pexels photo IDs.

Profiles created from stock photos are examples for a class project. They do not represent verified shelter animals or real availability.
